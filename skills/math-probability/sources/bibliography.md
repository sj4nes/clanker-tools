# Bibliography — Release 0.1

| key | reference |
|---|---|
| `kolmogorov_1933` | A. N. Kolmogorov, *Grundbegriffe der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung*, Springer 1933 (Eng. tr. *Foundations of the Theory of Probability*, Chelsea 1956). The three axioms. |
| `bayes_1763` | T. Bayes, "An Essay towards solving a Problem in the Doctrine of Chances", *Phil. Trans.* 53 (1763). |
| `billingsley_probability_measure` | P. Billingsley, *Probability and Measure*, 3rd ed., Wiley 1995. Primary source for the measure/integration bridge, the SLLN (§22), the CLT (§27), Lévy continuity (§26). |
| `durrett_pte` | R. Durrett, *Probability: Theory and Examples*, 5th ed., Cambridge 2019. Modes of convergence (ch. 2–3), CLT (Thm 3.4.1), conditional expectation (ch. 4). |
| `williams_probability_martingales` | D. Williams, *Probability with Martingales*, Cambridge 1991. Conditional expectation as an `L^2` projection (ch. 9), the tower property, a clean SLLN. |
| `grimmett_stirzaker` | G. Grimmett and D. Stirzaker, *Probability and Random Processes*, 4th ed., OUP 2020. Distribution families, generating functions, worked inequalities. |
| `jaynes_probability_theory` | E. T. Jaynes, *Probability Theory: The Logic of Science*, Cambridge 2003. Bayesian framing, Cox's theorem, common misuses. |
| `boucheron_lugosi_massart` | S. Boucheron, G. Lugosi, P. Massart, *Concentration Inequalities*, OUP 2013. Markov/Chernoff/Hoeffding and the sub-Gaussian toolkit. |
| `folland_real_analysis` | G. B. Folland, *Real Analysis*, 2nd ed., Wiley 1999. The cited measure theory: Carathéodory extension (§1.4), the integral (§2.2–2.4), Radon–Nikodym (§3.2), Fubini–Tonelli (§2.5). |
| `cinlar_probability_stochastics` | E. Çınlar, *Probability and Stochastics*, Springer 2011. π–λ theorem, transition kernels, the doorway to the boundary nodes. |
| `rudin_principles` | W. Rudin, *Principles of Mathematical Analysis*, 3rd ed., McGraw-Hill 1976. Cited for the analysis roots (ℝ, sequences, series, convexity) discharged into `math-real-analysis`. |
| `tao_analysis_I` | T. Tao, *Analysis I*, 4th ed., Hindustan Book Agency / Springer 2022. Same analysis-roots role. |
| `enderton_set_theory` | H. Enderton, *Elements of Set Theory*, Academic Press 1977. Cited for the set-algebra / preimage-algebra / countability roots discharged into `math-sets-functions-cardinality`. |
| `halmos_naive_set_theory` | P. Halmos, *Naive Set Theory*, Van Nostrand 1960. Same set-roots role. |
| `devroye_nonuniform` | L. Devroye, *Non-Uniform Random Variate Generation*, Springer 1986. The probability integral transform and inversion sampling. |
| `feller_vol1` | W. Feller, *An Introduction to Probability Theory and Its Applications, Vol. I*, 3rd ed., Wiley 1968. Memorylessness, discrete families, the renewal argument. |
| `vaart_asymptotic` | A. W. van der Vaart, *Asymptotic Statistics*, Cambridge 1998. The delta method, Slutsky, the continuous mapping theorem in statistical use. |
| `hoeffding_1963` | W. Hoeffding, "Probability inequalities for sums of bounded random variables", *JASA* 58 (1963). The lemma and the inequality. |
| `karatzas_shreve` | I. Karatzas and S. Shreve, *Brownian Motion and Stochastic Calculus*, 2nd ed., Springer 1991. Cited only by the boundary nodes. |
