# span_is_smallest_subspace

## Type
proposition

## Statement
span(S) is a subspace containing S, and it is contained in every subspace of V containing S. Equivalently, span(S) is the intersection of all subspaces containing S.

## Symbols
- `U` — any subspace containing S

## Epistemic status
proposition  ·  field_scope: any_field

## Prerequisites (tsort edges into this node)
span, subspace, subspace_criterion

## Hypotheses
U a subspace containing S

## Proof provenance
technique: closure of span(S) from the criterion; minimality because a subspace containing S is closed under the finite combinations that make up span(S), by induction on the number of terms
derives_from: subspace_criterion
lean_status: cited

## Type / well-formedness check
Well-formed. The two characterisations (constructive: finite combinations; and top-down: intersection of all containing subspaces) are proved equal, which is what licenses calling span(S) 'the subspace generated by S'.

## Specialization / boundary cases
- S a subspace already: then span(S) = S

## Hypothesis-dropped counterexamples
- **U_being_a_subspace**: the minimality claim fails for arbitrary supersets: S is contained in many sets that do not contain span(S)

## Common misuse
- using the intersection characterisation to compute a span: it is a definition-level equivalence, not an algorithm. Computation goes through gaussian_elimination
