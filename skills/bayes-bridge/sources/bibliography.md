# Bibliography

- [gelman_bda3] Gelman, Carlin, Stern, Dunson, Vehtari, Rubin, *Bayesian Data
  Analysis*, 3rd ed. — conjugate families (Ch. 2), model comparison (Ch. 7),
  credible-interval interpretation (§4.1).
- [casella_berger] Casella & Berger, *Statistical Inference*, 2nd ed. — §7.2.3
  Bayes estimators and conjugate priors.
- [kass_raftery_1995] Kass & Raftery, "Bayes Factors", *JASA* 90(430), 1995 —
  the Jeffreys scale, computation and interpretation of Bayes factors.
- [lindley_1957] Lindley, D.V., "A statistical paradox", *Biometrika* 44,
  1957 — the original paradox.
- [jeffreys_1961] Jeffreys, H., *Theory of Probability*, 3rd ed., §5.2 —
  the Jeffreys–Lindley phenomenon from the Bayesian side.
- [wasserman_all_of_statistics] Wasserman, *All of Statistics*, §11.9 —
  the flat-prior credible/confidence coincidence.
