# Kettle logic

A borrowed kettle comes back cracked, and three defences arrive at once: it was
whole when returned, it was cracked when borrowed, and no kettle was borrowed.
Each is fine on its own. Together they are an admission.

The same shape turns up in **postmortems**. Count the defences.
